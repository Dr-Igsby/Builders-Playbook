Step 04

Blind Spots, Traps & SWOT Analysis

Amateurs jump straight into building; pros stress-test their thinking. Force Claude to uncover what you are failing to consider.

Prompt 3: Blind Spots & Traps Interrogation 
Act as a ruthless technical auditor and senior systems architect. 

Review our project charter and constitution, then run a comprehensive interrogation:
1. **Blind Spots:** What critical factors am I failing to consider (technical bottlenecks, edge cases, user friction)?
2. **Hidden Traps:** What assumptions seem simple on paper but are dangerous rabbit holes to build?
3. **SWOT Matrix:** Give me a rigorous Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats breakdown.
4. **Pre-Mortem:** If this project fails or becomes abandoned in 2 weeks, what was the exact reason?

Be direct, candid, and challenge my assumptions.
